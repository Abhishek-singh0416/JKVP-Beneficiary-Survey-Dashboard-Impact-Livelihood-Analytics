# JKVP Beneficiary Survey Dashboard – Impact & Livelihood Analytics

## Zoho Analytics Based Beneficiary Survey & Impact Assessment Dashboard

---

# Project Overview

Developed a comprehensive **Beneficiary Survey & Impact Analytics Dashboard** in Zoho Analytics for monitoring livelihood impact, income improvement, employment transition, skill enhancement, and entrepreneurship outcomes of beneficiaries enrolled under the JKVP training program.

The dashboard transformed large-scale beneficiary survey data into a centralized analytical platform for:

* Program impact assessment
* Livelihood improvement analysis
* Gender-based performance insights
* Training effectiveness monitoring
* Employment outcome tracking
* Social category analysis
* Course performance evaluation

The solution enabled stakeholders to evaluate how training programs influenced employment, income generation, entrepreneurship, and quality of work among beneficiaries.

---

# Project Objective

The primary objective of the project was to:

* Measure program impact on beneficiaries
* Track income improvement after training
* Monitor employment transition
* Evaluate course effectiveness
* Analyze gender and social group impact
* Track entrepreneurship outcomes
* Measure quality of work improvements
* Provide centralized impact reporting

---

# Business Challenges

Before implementation:

* Survey data analysis was manual
* No centralized impact monitoring system existed
* Large datasets were difficult to interpret
* Stakeholders lacked real-time analytical insights
* Gender and social category comparisons were unavailable
* Program outcome reporting required extensive manual effort
* Measuring training effectiveness across states and partners was difficult

The organization required an analytics platform to monitor and evaluate the real-world impact of skill development programs.

---

# 2. Income Change Rate Analysis

Developed advanced income comparison analytics.

## Features

* Pre-training vs post-training income
* Gender-wise income comparison
* Course-wise income growth
* State-wise income improvement
* Training partner-wise income analysis

### Core Formula

Income\ Change\ % = \frac{Post\ Income - Prior\ Income}{Prior\ Income} \times 100

### Business Outcome

Enabled stakeholders to measure:

* Economic impact of training
* Livelihood improvement
* Course effectiveness
* Regional performance

Income change analysis across states, districts, and training partners was included in the dashboard.
